Our story

Built to connect classroom knowledge with real-world technology work.

Silicon Techno Solutions was started in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, with a clear purpose: to deliver dependable technology solutions while helping learners gain the practical skills that industry expects.

Over time, our work has grown to cover software development, technical training, internships, live projects, research and development, and support for technical research and publication. Each of these areas shares the same foundation — understanding real needs and applying knowledge to solve them.

Mission and vision

Mission

Empower people and organisations through practical technology.

To provide reliable technology solutions and industry-oriented learning — training, internships, live projects and research support — that help people build real skills and organisations solve real problems.

Vision

A trusted bridge between education and industry.

To be recognised as a trusted technology partner that bridges the gap between academic learning and industry practice, fostering innovation, research and skilled talent.
