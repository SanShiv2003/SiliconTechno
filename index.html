Who we are

A technology company focused on practical skills and working solutions.

Silicon Techno Solutions is a Guntur-based technology company working at the meeting point of industry and education.

We build software for organisations, and we prepare students and professionals for real work through training, internships, live projects and research guidance. Every engagement follows the same principle: understand the need, learn what is required, apply it to real problems and keep improving.

  • Industry-oriented learning — training and internships built around practical tasks.
  • End-to-end solutions — from requirement analysis to deployment.
  • Registered organisation — AICTE corporate and MSME Udyam registrations.

Our approach

Understand. Learn. Apply. Improve.

A simple four-stage method that guides every project, program and research engagement.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We start by understanding the problem, the learner or client, and the expected outcome before anything is built or taught.

  2. 02

    Learn

    We build the required knowledge — concepts, tools and techniques — with structured, practical learning.

  3. 03

    Apply

    Knowledge is put to work on real tasks, live projects and working solutions.

  4. 04

    Improve

    We review results, gather feedback and refine — so every outcome keeps getting better.
